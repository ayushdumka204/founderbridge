# MASTER PROMPT — PREMIUM STUDENT STARTUP + INVESTOR + SAAS + TECH PLATFORM

Design a complete, premium, original technology platform website that connects **students, investors, small businesses, and the technology ecosystem**.

The platform has four main purposes:

1. Students can submit and showcase their startup ideas.
2. Investors can discover student startup ideas, rate them, give feedback, save ideas, and potentially invest/connect with founders.
3. Small businesses can request custom SaaS software designed around their specific business needs.
4. Users can discover fresh, daily technology and startup news.

The website should feel like a **real modern technology startup product**, not a simple informational website.

---

# 1. MOST IMPORTANT — HUMAN-DESIGNED LOOK

The website must **NOT look AI-generated**.

It should look like a website created by an experienced senior product/UI designer for a real, well-funded technology startup.

Avoid:

* Generic AI landing-page layouts
* Generic SaaS templates
* Excessive gradients
* Excessive glassmorphism
* Random floating blobs
* Unnecessary decorative shapes
* Overuse of rounded cards
* Repetitive card grids
* Huge meaningless headings
* Too many colors
* Cheesy stock photography
* Artificial-looking illustrations
* Every section having the same structure
* Excessive shadows
* Over-designed UI

Every element should have a clear purpose.

The overall design should feel:

**Human + Premium + Modern + Trustworthy + Original + Intelligent + Professional**

# 5. CARD DESIGN — VERY IMPORTANT

Cards should be used **intentionally**, not everywhere.

Do not make every section a card.

Use cards primarily for:

* Startup ideas
* News
* Investors
* SaaS solutions
* Statistics
* Small information groups

Cards must have carefully designed dimensions.

Avoid extremely large cards that waste space.

Avoid tiny cards that make information difficult to read.

Use:

* Appropriate padding
* Consistent spacing
* Subtle borders
* Very subtle shadows
* Controlled corner radius
* Correct image ratios
* Strong content hierarchy

Use different card sizes when content requires it.

For example:

### News

One large featured story + two medium stories + smaller supporting stories.

### Startup

Medium-sized cards containing enough information without feeling crowded.

### Investor

Compact but informative profile cards.

### SaaS

Wider cards for feature explanations.
